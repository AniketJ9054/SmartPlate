02 Problem Statement

FA1 Baseline
College canteens face severe operational misalignment between daily food preparation and actual student consumption, resulting in 15–20 kg of edible food wasted daily per canteen while simultaneously causing frequent peak-hour stockouts for 30%+ of students.
Limitation 1: Intuitive Heuristic Cooking

Contractors cook on guesswork without headcount data, causing up to 25% overproduction on low-attendance days.

Limitation 2: Zero Demand Visibility

No pre-order mechanism exists; popular dishes sell out within 15 mins while secondary meals are dumped.

Evidence: 18.5 kg daily cooked food discarded at closing time; 72% students left hungry 2x/week.

06 Framework: Google Sprint & Stanford d.school

Carries 10 Marks

Google Design Sprint + 5-Stage Design Thinking

5-Day Iterative Sprint Cycle
Why This Framework Suits SmartPlate: Campus food waste is an operational wicked problem with tight human friction (time-starved students vs labor-constrained kitchen operators). Rapid sprint cycles enabled rapid hypothesis validation, early field testing, and quick pivot away from rigid features (like cutoff deadlines) without wasting development overhead.
Sprint D1 Empathize

Audited PCCOE canteen bins; surveyed 68 campus users; conducted empathy interviews with students and chef Ramesh.

Output: Empathy Maps & Canteen Journey Touchpoints
Sprint D2 Define

Synthesized 32 user observations into 8 Affinity Clusters; uncovered the paradox connecting overproduction with food stockouts.

Output: 8-Cluster Affinity Diagram & Crisp HMW Statement
Sprint D3 Ideate

Brainstormed 20+ ideas via Crazy 8s; prioritized on an Impact-Feasibility Matrix; selected dynamic pre-booking with heuristic multiplier.

Output: Predictive Algorithm Flow & System Architecture
Sprint D4 Prototype

Engineered a functional full-stack web application in Flask & SQLite: Student Pre-Booking Portal, Kitchen Ops, and Waste Analytics Hub.

Output: Live Working Web Application (SmartPlate MVP)
Sprint D5 Test

Field-tested with 15 PCCOE students and 2 canteen staff; discovered deadline friction and unified admin credentials in real time.

Output: Validated Feedback, Refined UX & Zero-Deadline MVP

08 Testing, Feedback & Iterations

Agile Loops
Tested with 15 PCCOE Students + 2 Canteen Operational Personnel
What Worked

• 100% students completed meal reservation in <30 seconds.
• Kitchen staff loved direct prep target numbers.

What Failed

• 10 AM booking cutoff failed; student schedules shifted.
• Split staff/admin logins caused canteen friction.

What We Changed

• Removed order deadlines across all meals.
• Unified admin/staff login (admin@pccoe.edu).
• Cleaned sample clutter.
