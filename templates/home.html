How It Works

Move from guesswork to data-backed canteen planning

1. Students pre-book meals

Students reserve breakfast, lunch, or dinner in advance based on the live daily menu.

2. SmartPlate estimates demand

Historical demand, bookings, and day-specific adjustments guide kitchen preparation targets.

3. Canteen prepares recommended portions

Staff align food production to real student demand rather than overproducing.

4. Staff log leftovers & improve accuracy

Daily leftover audits continuously train prediction models and prevent food wastage.

Core Features

Designed for PCCOE Campus Dining

Student Pre-Booking

Easy reservation of breakfast, lunch, and dinner to secure meal availability.

Statistical Forecasting

Transparent predictive formulas combine prior waste, confirmed bookings, and weekday weights.

Live Order Fulfillment

Kitchen staff can view incoming reservations and mark meals as served in real time.

Waste Tracking Audit

Log leftovers by food item and cause to take targeted corrective actions.

Executive Analytics

Review daily waste trends, popular dishes, cost savings, and downloadable audit CSV reports.

Unified Management

Consolidated administration for both kitchen food prep and administrative auditing.
