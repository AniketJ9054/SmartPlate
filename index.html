How It Works

Move from guesswork to data-backed canteen planning

1. Students pre-book meals

Students reserve breakfast, lunch, or snacks in advance based on the live daily canteen menu.

2. SmartPlate estimates demand

Historical demand, current bookings, and day-of-week multipliers calculate exact preparation targets.

3. Canteen prepares recommended portions

Kitchen staff align batch cooking to real demand, eliminating morning overproduction.

4. Staff log leftovers & improve accuracy

Daily leftover audits continuously calibrate forecasting formulas to prevent food wastage.

Core Features

Designed for PCCOE Campus Dining

Student Pre-Booking

Statistical Forecasting

Transparent predictive formulas combine prior waste rates, advance bookings, and weekday weights.

Live Order Fulfillment
